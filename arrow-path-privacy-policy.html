Last updated: October 8, 2026

  • Device information

Arrow Path may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information about your device and interaction with advertisements to provide, measure, and improve advertising.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

Arrow Path may use third-party services including:

These providers may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

Arrow Path does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

If the app is intended for children or targets children, our Google Play target-audience and content declarations will accurately reflect that intended audience.

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the app. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Arrow Path does not intentionally store personal information on our own servers unless required for a specific app feature. Information collected by third-party services may be retained according to their respective privacy policies.

Depending on your device and location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. You can also manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google account.
